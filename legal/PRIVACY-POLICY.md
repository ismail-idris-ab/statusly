This policy explains what happens to information when you use Statusly. It applies only to the Statusly Android app and not to any other service.

## 2. The short version

We built Statusly to work almost entirely on your device.

- **We do not have a server, and you do not create an account.**
- **Your status photos and videos never leave your phone.** We do not upload, store, transmit, back up, or look at them.
- **We do not sell your personal data.**
- The only information that leaves your device is what our **advertising** and **crash-reporting** providers collect — mainly your advertising ID, IP address, and general device information.
- If you buy "Remove Ads", **Google Play handles the payment.** We never see your card or billing details.

The rest of this policy explains the detail.

## 3. Information we do NOT collect

To be explicit, Statusly does **not** collect, transmit, or store on any server:

- Your status photos or videos, or any media you save
- Your WhatsApp messages, chats, or call history
- Your phone contacts or address book
- Your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth
- Your precise or approximate location
- Any account credentials
- Any payment or card details

## 4. Information processed only on your device

Statusly needs to read and organise certain information to work. All of the following is stored **locally on your device only** and is never sent to us or anyone else:

| What | Why | Where it's stored |
|------|-----|-------------------|
| Status media files (photos/videos) that WhatsApp has already saved to your device | To display statuses so you can view, save, share, or repost them | Read in place from the folder you grant access to; temporary copies in the app's private cache when sharing |
| Media you choose to save | So you can keep and re-share it | Your device gallery and/or the App's private storage |
| An index of saved items (file reference, type, size, date) | To power the "Saved" library | Local database on your device |
| Status groupings and "already seen" markers | To group statuses and detect new ones for alerts | Local database on your device |
| Your settings (alerts on/off, theme, ad-free status, onboarding state) | To remember your preferences | Local storage on your device |

**Important:** Statusly reads status files that **WhatsApp itself has already downloaded to your device** when you viewed those statuses. Statusly does not connect to WhatsApp's servers, does not log into your WhatsApp account, and cannot access statuses you have not already viewed in WhatsApp.

Deleting the App removes all of this local data. Media you deliberately saved to your device gallery remains, because it belongs to you — you can delete it yourself at any time.

## 5. Information collected by third-party services

Statusly includes a small number of third-party services. These providers may collect information directly from your device under their own privacy policies. We do not control their processing.

### 5.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

If you have not purchased "Remove Ads", we show advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process:

- Your **advertising ID** (a resettable identifier)
- Your **IP address** and approximate, IP-derived location (typically country/city level)
- **Device and app information** (device model, operating system version, app version, language, network type)
- **Ad interaction data** (ads shown, viewability, clicks)

This is used to serve, measure, and improve ads, and — where permitted — to personalise ads.
Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · Partner list: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

### 5.2 Google Play Billing (in-app purchase)

If you purchase "Remove Ads", the transaction is processed entirely by **Google Play**. We receive only a confirmation that an entitlement exists on your Google account. We never receive or store your payment card, bank details, or billing address.

### 5.3 Sentry (crash and error reporting)

To find and fix crashes, we use Sentry. When the App crashes or hits an error, Sentry may receive:

- A technical error report (stack trace, the app screen or code path involved)
- Device model, operating system version, app version
- Your IP address (used for delivery; we configure Sentry to avoid storing it where possible)

Crash reports are technical diagnostics. They do not include your status media, saved files, or message content.
Sentry's policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

### 5.4 Notifications

"New status alerts" are generated **locally on your device**. Statusly does not use a push notification server and does not send your device a push token or any identifier to us.

## 6. Permissions we request, and why

We ask for the minimum needed. You can refuse or revoke any of these in your device settings, though some features will stop working.

| Permission / access | Why we need it | If you decline |
|---------------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Folder access to the WhatsApp status folder** (granted by you through Android's system folder picker) | The core function: to read the status photos and videos WhatsApp has already stored on your device so you can view and save them | The App cannot show statuses and will not function |
| **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) | To alert you when a new status appears, if you turn alerts on | You get no alerts; everything else works |
| **Run at startup** (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) | To restart the background alert check after your phone reboots | Alerts stop working after a reboot |
| **Internet / network state** | Only to load advertisements and send crash reports | Ads fail to load; the App otherwise works offline |
| **Advertising ID** (`AD_ID`) | Used by AdMob to serve and measure ads | Ads become non-personalised |
| **Photo & video access** (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`) | To save statuses into your device gallery and to display the items in your "Saved" library | You cannot save statuses to your gallery |

**We do not request access to your contacts, your location, your camera, your microphone, your call logs, or your SMS messages.**

## 7. How information is used

Information is used only to:

- Show you the statuses on your device and let you save, share, or repost them
- Maintain your saved library and your settings
- Detect new statuses and notify you, if you enable alerts
- Display advertisements and confirm whether you have removed them
- Diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance

We do **not** use your information for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do **not** make automated decisions about you.

## 8. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Information is shared only in these limited situations:

- **With the service providers named in Section 5** (Google AdMob, Google Play, Sentry), for the purposes described there.
- **When you choose to share.** If you tap Share or Repost, Android hands the file to the app you pick (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.). That app then handles it under *its* privacy policy, not ours.
- **Legal obligation.** If required by valid law, regulation, or court order, or to protect our rights, safety, or property. In practice, we hold almost no data that could be disclosed.
- **Business transfer.** If the App is ever sold or transferred, any data would move with it, subject to this policy.

## 9. Advertising and your choices

You have real control over advertising:

- **Remove ads entirely** by purchasing the one-time "Remove Ads" option in the App.
- **Reset or delete your advertising ID** in *Settings → Google → Ads* on your Android device.
- **Opt out of ad personalisation** using *Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation*. You will still see ads, but less relevant ones.
- **If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland**, we present a consent message before serving personalised ads, using a Google-certified consent management platform. You can change your choice at any time in the App's Settings.

## 10. Data retention and deletion

- **Local data** (saved index, settings, groupings) stays on your device until you delete it in the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.
- **Saved media** stays in your device gallery until you delete it. It is yours.
- **Cached copies** made for sharing are temporary and cleared automatically.
- **Third-party data** (ads, crash reports) is retained by those providers under their own retention schedules — see their policies in Section 5.

Because Statusly has no accounts and no server, **there is no account for us to delete and no server-side copy of your data to erase.** To remove everything associated with the App, uninstall it and, if you wish, delete any media you saved.

## 11. Children's privacy

Statusly is intended for users aged **13 and over**, and our Google Play target audience is declared accordingly. The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the App in a way that provided personal data to our advertising or crash-reporting providers, please contact us and we will help you exercise your rights with those providers.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, note that the age of digital consent varies by country (13 to 16). Where you are below that age, a parent or guardian should review this policy and consent on your behalf.

## 12. Your rights

### 12.1 Nigeria (NDPA)

If you are in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 gives you rights over personal data relating to you, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**.

### 12.2 European Economic Area and United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, to object to processing (including to direct marketing and to profiling for advertising), and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also complain to your national supervisory authority.

**Legal bases we rely on:**

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---------|-------------|
| Running the App's core features on your device | Performance of a contract (providing the App you requested) |
| Personalised advertising | Your consent, collected via a Google-certified consent platform |
| Non-personalised advertising | Legitimate interests (funding a free app), balanced against your rights |
| Crash and error reporting | Legitimate interests (keeping the App secure and stable) |

### 12.3 California (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident you may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected, request deletion or correction, and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. **We do not sell personal information.** Sharing of advertising identifiers for cross-context behavioural advertising may qualify as "sharing" under the CPRA — you can opt out using the controls in Section 9. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

### 12.4 How to exercise your rights

Email us at the address in Section 1. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally 30 days). We may need to ask you for information to verify your request. Because we hold virtually no data that identifies you, we may direct you to Google or Sentry for data held by them, and we will help you do so.

## 13. International data transfers

We do not transfer your data ourselves, because we do not collect it on a server. Our third-party providers (Google, Sentry) operate globally and may process data outside your country, including in the United States. They rely on legal transfer mechanisms such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. See their privacy policies for detail.

## 14. Security

Because your data stays on your device, the main protection is your device's own security — your screen lock, encryption, and OS updates. In addition:

- The App uses Android's scoped storage and the Storage Access Framework, so it can only read the specific folder you explicitly grant.
- Temporary cached files are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
- Network connections for ads and crash reporting use encrypted HTTPS/TLS.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
